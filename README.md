## 🎯 Key Features

1. **Deterministic Zero-LLM Analysis Engine**:
   - Analyzes response latency asymmetry, hedging markers (*"should be"*, *"idk"*), DARVO deflection maneuvers (*"you're being a bit much"*), and cold one-word bombs (*"ok"*, *"k"*).
2. **Instant Sample Relationships (12 Chat Dynamics)**:
   - The Situationship, The Friend Group, Your Relationship, The Family Group, Your Ex, The Work Chat, Your Crush, Your Best Friend, The Talking Stage, Long-Distance, The New One, and Married.
3. **Multi-Platform Support**:
   - Supports exports from WhatsApp (`.txt`), Instagram / Messenger (`.json`), iMessage (`.csv` / text), LINE, and KakaoTalk.
4. **Editorial Report Dossier**:
   - Full multi-paragraph narrative verdict with clickable timestamped evidence chips.
   - Cast character profiles, unspoken motives, and superlative awards (*Chief Hedge Architect*, *Coldest 2-Letter Drop*).
   - "What They Wrote vs. What They Actually Meant" subtext decoder.
5. **High-DPI Shareable Story Cards**:
   - Generates high-resolution parchment story card PNGs directly via HTML5 Canvas for sharing to group chats.
